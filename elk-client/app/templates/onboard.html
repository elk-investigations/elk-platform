Investigative Services Agreement — Summary

ELK Investigations LLC ("ELK") agrees to provide investigative services as described in this intake. Services are rendered on a retainer basis and billed at ELK's standard rates. All findings are confidential and protected as investigative work product.

Client acknowledges that: (1) investigators observe and document factual information only and do not provide legal conclusions or opinions; (2) investigation results are not guaranteed; (3) ELK operates in full compliance with NYS Private Investigator licensing law (GBL Article 7) and all applicable federal and state statutes; (4) client has a lawful purpose for requesting investigative services; (5) this summary does not replace the full Engagement Letter, which will be provided separately prior to commencement of fieldwork.

By signing below, client authorizes ELK Investigations LLC to commence the requested investigation and agrees to pay all fees associated with services rendered. Fees are earned upon service delivery and are non-refundable absent written agreement.

For attorney-referred matters: this agreement constitutes an engagement between ELK Investigations LLC and the referring attorney. All work product is prepared in anticipation of litigation and is protected under the work-product doctrine. Findings will be delivered to counsel, not directly to the underlying client, unless otherwise instructed in writing.
